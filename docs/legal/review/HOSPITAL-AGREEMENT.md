## 1. Purpose

1.1 The Partner refers its patients who need physiotherapy to MoveRestore,
which provides that physiotherapy by video consultation or at the patient's
home through its platform. This agreement sets out how referrals work, how the
Partner is paid, and how patient data is protected.

1.2 Each party stays independent. Neither may bind the other.

## 2. Referrals

2.1 The Partner refers a patient through its partner account on the platform
("**Partner Portal**"), entering only the details needed: the patient's name,
phone number, address (for a home visit), preferred language, medical issue
and the treatment needed.

2.2 Before referring, the Partner will obtain the patient's **consent** to
share these details with MoveRestore for arranging physiotherapy, and keep a
record of that consent. *[Lawyer: consider whether MoveRestore should obtain
its own consent at the invite link as well -- the platform already asks the
patient to accept MoveRestore's Terms and Privacy policy when they create
their account from the invite.]*

2.3 MoveRestore will review each referral within [2 working days], and either
assign a therapist and send the patient an invitation, or decline it with a
reason shown on the Partner Portal.

2.4 Clinical decisions about physiotherapy are MoveRestore's therapists'.
The Partner remains responsible for the medical care it provides.

## 3. Commission

3.1 For each **completed** session delivered to a patient the Partner
referred, MoveRestore pays the Partner a commission of **[__]%** of the
session's **net revenue** (the price the patient paid, after discounts and
refunds), as set on the Partner's account when the session is completed.

3.2 A session refunded to the patient earns no commission; a commission
already paid on it is deducted from the next statement.

3.3 MoveRestore pays commission [monthly], within [15] days of the month's
end, by bank transfer, with a statement listing each session (by reference,
not by patient name) and the amount. The Partner Portal shows the same figures
as they accrue.

3.4 The Partner will issue a tax invoice for each payment if it is GST
registered. *[Lawyer/accountant: confirm GST treatment of referral commission
for a healthcare provider, and whether it raises any concern under the
medical-ethics rules on fee-splitting for referrals.]*

## 4. Data protection

4.1 For the referral details in 2.1, the Partner is the source and
MoveRestore becomes a **Data Fiduciary** for its own use of them under the
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, once the patient accepts its
invitation. Each party is responsible for its own compliance.

4.2 MoveRestore shares back with the Partner only: whether the referral was
accepted, declined or withdrawn; the status of sessions; and the commission
figures. MoveRestore does **not** share the patient's health profile,
session notes or documents with the Partner unless the patient asks it to in
writing.

4.3 Each party will keep the other's patient data confidential, use it only
for this agreement, protect it with reasonable security, and tell the other
**within 24 hours** (and in time for the 6-hour CERT-In report where it
applies) of any breach affecting it.

4.4 The Partner's staff using the Partner Portal will each use their own
sign-in and keep it private. The Partner is responsible for their actions.

## 5. Brand

Each party may name the other as a partner on its website and materials, using
the other's logo only as supplied and approved by it.

## 6. Term and ending

6.1 This agreement runs for [12 months] from the effective date and renews
for further [12-month] periods unless either party gives [60] days' notice
before renewal.

6.2 Either party may end it immediately on written notice if the other
seriously breaches it and does not put it right within [15] days of being
asked.

6.3 On ending: referrals already accepted continue to be treated;
commission on sessions completed before the end is paid; and clauses 4 and 8
continue.

## 7. Responsibility

Each party is responsible for its own services, staff and compliance.
*[Lawyer: mutual indemnities and a liability cap.]*

## 8. Disputes and law

8.1 The parties will first try to settle a dispute by discussion between
senior representatives within [30] days.

8.2 This agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at
**[CITY]** have jurisdiction. *[Lawyer: consider arbitration.]*

### Notes for the reviewing lawyer

- **Referral commission.** A commission to a hospital for referring patients
  may raise professional-ethics or anti-kickback concerns for the hospital's
  doctors. Please advise whether to restructure it (for example, as a fee for
  coordination services).
- **Data roles.** Drafted on the basis that each party is an independent Data
  Fiduciary. Please confirm, and whether a data sharing annex is needed.
- **Blank fields** in [square brackets] are business decisions or facts the
  owner will supply.

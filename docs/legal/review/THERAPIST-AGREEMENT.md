## 1. What this agreement is

1.1 MoveRestore runs a platform through which patients book physiotherapy
sessions by video and, where offered, at home. You provide those sessions as
an **independent professional**. This agreement sets out how.

1.2 Nothing here makes you an employee, partner or agent of MoveRestore. You
are responsible for your own income tax, GST (if applicable) and professional
obligations.

## 2. Your qualifications

2.1 You confirm that you hold a recognised physiotherapy qualification and a
current registration to practise in India, and that you will keep it current.

2.2 You will tell us within **48 hours** if your registration is suspended,
restricted or withdrawn, or if any complaint or proceeding is started against
you by a council, court or patient.

2.3 You will hold **professional indemnity insurance** of at least
[AMOUNT] for the whole term, and give us a copy on request.
*[Lawyer: confirm whether required, and the amount.]*

2.4 The details you give at signup (credentials, specialisation, years of
experience, languages, bio) must be true. We may verify them before approving
your account.

## 3. Providing sessions

3.1 You choose your weekly availability on the platform. Sessions are offered
to patients only in the hours you set, and you may take leave by marking it on
the platform.

3.2 For each session assigned to you, you will:

a) join video sessions on time through the link the platform provides, from a
private place, and never record a session without the patient's written
agreement;

b) for home visits, arrive on time at the address given, carry identification,
and follow the safety and conduct standards in Schedule 1;

c) assess and treat the patient with reasonable professional skill and care,
within the scope of physiotherapy practice, and refer the patient to a doctor
or emergency care when their condition requires it;

d) record the session's notes, care plan and summary on the platform within
[24] hours, completely and accurately.

3.3 If you cannot attend a session, you will tell us through the platform as
early as possible, and at least [12] hours before, except in an emergency.
Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may lead to suspension (clause 10).

3.4 You will not ask for, or accept, payment from a patient other than through
the platform, except cash collected at a home visit where the platform records
it (clause 5.4).

## 4. Patients are MoveRestore's patients

4.1 During this agreement and for **[12] months** after it ends, you will not
directly or indirectly offer paid services to a patient you first met through
the platform, or invite them to book with you outside it.
*[Lawyer: confirm enforceability under s.27 Indian Contract Act; consider a
narrower restriction limited to soliciting during the term.]*

4.2 You will not share your personal phone number, email or social media with
patients through the platform. The platform blocks and records attempts to do
so.

## 5. Fees and payouts

5.1 For each **completed** session you deliver, you earn your **revenue share**
of the session's net price (after any discount), as set on your account at the
time the session is completed: **[__]%** for video sessions and **[__]%** for
home visits. For home visits you also receive the **travel fee in full**.

5.2 A session that is cancelled and refunded earns nothing. A patient no-show
earns [the full share / __% / nothing]. *[Owner to decide.]*

5.3 We pay your earnings by [bank transfer / UPI] [weekly / fortnightly /
monthly, or on your payout request through the platform], with a statement
showing each session and amount. Payment is made within [7] days of the end of
each payout period.

5.4 Cash collected at a home visit belongs to MoveRestore. You hold it for us
and it is deducted from your next payout; if it exceeds your earnings, you
will pay the difference within [7] days.

5.5 We may deduct from your payouts any amount we refunded to a patient
because of your error or misconduct, after telling you why. You may dispute it
under clause 12.

5.6 Your revenue share may change on [30] days' written notice. A change never
affects sessions already completed.

## 6. Confidentiality and patient data

6.1 Patients' personal and health information is **sensitive personal data**.
MoveRestore is the Data Fiduciary for it under the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023; you process it **only on our behalf and only to treat
the patient**.

6.2 You will:

a) access only the patients assigned to you, and only what you need;

b) not copy, download, photograph or store patient data outside the platform,
except as the law requires;

c) keep your sign-in private, use a device with a screen lock, and sign out of
shared devices;

d) tell us **immediately, and within 6 hours at the latest,** if you suspect
any patient data was lost, seen by someone else or misused (see our breach
response plan);

e) on the end of this agreement, return or delete any patient data you hold
outside the platform.

6.3 You will keep confidential MoveRestore's business information (prices,
patient lists, processes, the platform itself).

6.4 These duties continue after this agreement ends.

## 7. Conduct

7.1 You will follow the professional code of conduct of your registering
council, and Schedule 1. Harassment, discrimination, or any sexual or
inappropriate conduct towards a patient ends this agreement immediately.

7.2 For a patient under 18, you will treat them only with their parent or
guardian present (on the call or in the home).

## 8. Content

8.1 Exercise plans, notes and materials you create on the platform for
MoveRestore's patients may be used by MoveRestore to provide care to those
patients. You keep any general know-how you bring.

8.2 You allow MoveRestore to show your name, photograph, qualifications and
bio on its website while this agreement is in force.

## 9. Responsibility

9.1 You are professionally responsible for the clinical care you give.

9.2 You will indemnify MoveRestore against claims arising from your
negligence, breach of this agreement, or breach of law, except to the extent
caused by MoveRestore.

9.3 MoveRestore is responsible for the platform, payments and its own staff.
*[Lawyer: liability caps for both sides.]*

## 10. Term, suspension and ending

10.1 This agreement starts on the effective date and continues until ended.

10.2 Either of us may end it on **[30]** days' written notice.

10.3 MoveRestore may suspend your account immediately, and end this agreement
on written notice, if you breach clauses 2, 4, 6 or 7, or if a patient's
safety is at risk.

10.4 When it ends: sessions already booked are reassigned or cancelled; we pay
everything you earned up to the end, less amounts under clauses 5.4 and 5.5;
and clauses 4, 6, 9 and 12 continue.

## 11. Changes

We may update this agreement on [30] days' notice through the platform. If you
do not agree, you may end it under clause 10.2 before the change applies.

## 12. Disputes and law

12.1 We will first try to settle any dispute by talking, within [30] days.

12.2 This agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at
**[CITY]** have jurisdiction. *[Lawyer: consider arbitration.]*

### Notes for the reviewing lawyer

- **Classification.** The intent is an independent contractor relationship
  (no salary, own schedule, revenue share). Please confirm this holds under
  current labour codes, and whether GST registration is needed above a
  threshold.
- **Non-solicitation (cl. 4.1).** Post-term restraints are generally void
  under s.27 Indian Contract Act; please advise on a defensible version.
- **Data processing (cl. 6).** Drafted on the basis that MoveRestore is the
  Data Fiduciary and the therapist acts on its behalf under DPDP. Please
  confirm, and whether a separate data processing annex is advisable.
- **Regulator.** Physiotherapists fall under the National Commission for
  Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 2021; state council registration
  varies. Please confirm the registration wording.
- **Blank fields** in [square brackets] are business decisions or facts the
  owner will supply.
